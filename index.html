Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before we turn your story into a song.

How much does a personalized song cost?

A personalized song costs $29.99. One change is included when requested within 5 days of receiving your song.

How long does it take to receive my song?

You’ll receive your personalized song by email within 24 hours after payment and submission of your story.

What details should I include in my story?

The more details you share, the more personalized your song will be! Include names, favorite memories, inside jokes, personality traits, and anything that makes the person or occasion special.

What music styles can I choose?

Choose from country, pop, acoustic / singer-songwriter, rock, R&B / soul, Christian / inspirational, or folk. You can also select “Surprise me” or “Other” and tell us the style you have in mind.

Can you make funny songs?

Yes! We can create heartfelt, funny, uplifting, or playful songs to fit the person and occasion.

What occasions can I order a song for?

Birthdays, weddings, anniversaries, memorials, holidays, family celebrations, or just because.

How will I receive my song?

We’ll email you an MP3 file you can download, keep, and share.

Can I request a change?

One change is included when requested within 5 days of receiving your song.
