MAKE IT PERSONAL

What should you tell us?

The more details you provide, the more personalized the birthday song can be.

The birthday person

Share their name, nickname, age if you want it mentioned, and your relationship. Tell us how to pronounce any unusual names.

The details only you know

Include hobbies, favorite sayings, funny habits, inside jokes, and a memory that always makes you smile. For a funny song, tell us which topics to avoid.

The feeling you want

Choose funny, heartfelt, happy, or playful. Pick country, pop, rock, acoustic, or another style from the order form, and list anything you want included.

Birthday song questions

Can the song be funny?

Yes! Choose “Fun & playful” and share the jokes or memories you want included. Let us know any sensitive topics to avoid.

When will it arrive?

Your song will arrive by email within 24 hours after payment and submission of your story. Allow time to download it and check your speaker before the celebration.

Can I request a change?

One change is included when requested within 5 days of receiving your song.

Do I have to write the lyrics?

No. Share your story and must-include details, and we’ll shape them into a personalized song.
