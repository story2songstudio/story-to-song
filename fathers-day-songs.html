Common questions

Can you make a funny song for Dad?

Yes. Share his sayings, harmless quirks, and inside jokes. Tell us which topics to avoid so the humor fits your family.

Can it be for Grandpa or a stepdad?

Yes. Tell us who he is to you and the name or title you use for him. We’ll build the song around the details you share.

How do I select this occasion?

Choose “Mother’s Day / Father’s Day” or “Other” in the order form, then describe the occasion and the person in your story.

When will the song arrive?

We’ll email your MP3 within 24 hours after payment and submission of your story. Allow time to download it and check playback before your celebration.

Can I request a change?

One change is included when requested within 5 days of receiving your song.
