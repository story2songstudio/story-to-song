What chapters belong in your song?

Your beginning

Share how you met, your first date, and what drew you to each other. Include names, nicknames, and pronunciation.

Your life together

Tell us about the wedding, family, trips, everyday traditions, and challenges you’ve faced together. Include the anniversary year if you want it mentioned.

What you love today

Describe the little things you appreciate, an inside joke, or a promise for the future. Choose a mood and music style that fits your relationship.

For your partner or a couple you love

Create a song from you to your partner, or give one to parents, grandparents, or friends celebrating their years together. Tell us who the gift is from and whose perspective the lyrics should use.

Common questions

Can you include how many years we’ve been together?

Yes. Add the number of years, important dates, and any milestones you want mentioned in the must-include details.

Can it be funny as well as romantic?

Yes. Share the jokes and playful memories you want included, and tell us which topics to avoid.

Do I need to write the lyrics?

No. Share your memories and the message you want to express, and we’ll shape them into a personalized song.

How will I receive the song?

We’ll email your MP3 within 24 hours after payment and submission of your story. You can download, keep, and share it.

Can I request a change?

One change is included when requested within 5 days of receiving your song.
