Common questions

Can you include more than one child?

Yes. List each child’s name, pronunciation, and favorite details, and explain whether they should share one song or whether the song is mainly about one child.

Can you include funny sounds or sayings?

Share the sounds, catchphrases, and playful moments you would like included, such as “vroom vroom” for a truck-loving child.

Can the song be gentle instead of energetic?

Yes. Choose a gentle mood and describe the feeling you want, such as a sweet family keepsake or a calm song to enjoy together.

How will I receive the song?

We’ll email your MP3 within 24 hours after payment and submission of your story. You can download, keep, and share it.

Can I request a change?

One change is included when requested within 5 days of receiving your song.
