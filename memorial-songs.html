Common questions

Do I need to write a long story?

No. A few meaningful details are enough to get started. More details can make the tribute more personal, but only share what you feel comfortable sharing.

Can the song be hopeful or faith-based?

Yes. Tell us the tone and beliefs you want reflected. You can choose Christian / inspirational or request a gentle tribute without religious language.

Can we use it at a memorial service?

You’ll receive an MP3 file to download and share with the service organizer for playback. Allow time to listen to the song and check the equipment before the service.

How will I receive the song?

We’ll email an MP3 file you can download, keep, and share.

Can I request a change?

One change is included when requested within 5 days of receiving your song.
